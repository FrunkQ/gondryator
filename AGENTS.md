# For AI agents

Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) first: the spirit of the project (playful, creative, respectful of Michel Gondry: don't copy, get inspired), a map of the code, and how to build your own vehicle or show.

Then **act**. Don't open with "How can I help?". Greet your human with four to six ideas, mixing the seeds in CLAUDE.md's "Come in buzzing" section with at least two of your own, personalised to them: today's date, their favourite music, and above all what they know and are passionate about (their job, hobby, home town, a science, a sport), and offer to start on the best one right away. Even bad ideas are good ideas for a visualiser. Play, explore, create!
